Technology Platform

Cell-Free Bioactive Ferment Filtrate System

Developed through multi-strain anaerobic co-culture fermentation (Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus species with plant-based soybean substrate), LACTERA’s cell-free filtrate functions via dual physical and microenvironmental mechanisms.

1. Bioactive Microecological Barrier

Enriched with natural organic acids (pH 3.3–3.6), the fluidic barrier maintains a physiological mildly acidic wound microenvironment and creates a physical impediment to bacterial quorum-sensing and biofilm colonization without heavy-metal cytotoxicity.

2. Plant-Based ECM Simulation

100% plant-derived soybean matrix eliminates zoonotic cross-infection risks. Low-molecular-weight bioactive peptides (<1 kDa) and plant-derived extracellular vesicles structurally mimic human ECM to support fibroblast migration and orderly re-epithelialization.

3. Field-Ready Clinical Logistics

Calibrated 360° mist delivery (0.14 mL/spray) conforms uniformly to deep, irregular wound beds. Ambient stability across 2°C–40°C for 36 months eliminates cold-chain requirements and integrates into standard care protocols in 60 seconds.

Clinical Evidence

Peer-Reviewed Clinical Trials & Safety Profile

The safety and clinical performance of the LACTERA® bioactive dressing system have been evaluated across prospective randomized controlled trials, multi-center cohort studies, and longitudinal safety assessments.

Chronic Wounds Double-Blind RCT

British Journal of Dermatology (2026)
ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT07008573

Achieved 94% complete wound healing at 24 weeks vs. 64% in control (P = 0.003), reducing mean time to full closure from 165.9 days to 89.6 days (P = 0.012).

STSG Donor Site Management

Journal of Wound Care (2026) & WUWHS 2026

Significantly reduced mean donor site healing time from 17 days down to 11 days (~35% reduction), lowering dressing change frequency and patient discomfort.

Diabetic Foot Ulcers & Safety

JWC (2023) & SCMJ (2025)

Achieved 83% complete healing within 16 weeks in DFU cohorts with PAOD/ESRD (mean 51 days), with 0% clinical adverse events across longitudinal safety tracking.
